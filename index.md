# Breezy Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 29 October 2026

Breezy is a quit smoking app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Murat Can Yuksel, an individual developer ("I", "me").

**The short version:** your smoking progress stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. The only data that leaves your device is the record of a purchase, if you subscribe.

## Who is responsible for your data

Murat Can Yuksel is the data controller for the processing described here.
Contact: [muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com](mailto:muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com)

## Data that stays on your device

- **Your progress.** Your answers in setup, your smoke-free days, breaks, slips, savings, level and settings are stored only inside the app on your device. They are never uploaded, and I never see them.
- **Reminders.** If you turn on reminders, Breezy schedules them on your device. They are not sent from a server.
- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login, so there is no account to delete.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your progress is not backed up to a Breezy account.

## Data that leaves your device: your subscription

When you subscribe or restore a purchase:

- **Apple** (or Google on Android) handles the payment. I never receive your name, your payment details or your store account. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to that.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** (United States) checks with the store whether you have an active subscription, so the app can unlock and you can restore it on another device. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID that the app creates (not your name, email or store account), your purchase history for this app (product, date, price, currency and the transaction details the store provides), and the technical details needed for that check, such as the app version, operating system version and store country. Like any internet connection, it also reveals your IP address to RevenueCat's servers.

I use this data to unlock and restore your subscription, to prevent fraud, and to see total sales in RevenueCat's dashboard. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising or tracking, and not sold. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**Legal basis (EU and UK GDPR):** performance of the contract you entered by subscribing (Article 6(1)(b)), and my legitimate interest in preventing fraud and understanding total sales (Article 6(1)(f)).

**Transfers outside the EU:** RevenueCat stores this data on servers in the United States and processes it on my behalf under its [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa), which uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Rating request

Breezy may ask you once to rate it, using the system rating prompt from Apple or Google. I do not receive any data from it.

## Emails you send me

If you write to me, I receive your email address and your message. I use them only to answer you and delete them within 12 months of our last exchange.

## Your rights

You can ask me, at the address above, what I hold about you, and to correct or delete it. I hold nothing about your progress. If you think I handled data wrongly, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Breezy is for adults. It is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top.
