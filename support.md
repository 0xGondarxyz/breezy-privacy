## Common questions

**Where is my progress stored?**
Only on your device, inside the app. It is not uploaded or synced. Deleting the app deletes it.

**I smoked. Did I lose everything?**
No. Tap **I smoked** and Breezy keeps your totals and your level, and starts a new smoke-free streak.

**How do I get my subscription on a new phone?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Breezy and tap **Restore Purchases** on the plan screen or in Settings.

**How do I cancel?**
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, pick Breezy and cancel. Or tap **Manage Subscription** in Breezy's Settings.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Reminders do not arrive.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Breezy in your phone's Settings, and that reminders are on in Breezy's Settings.
